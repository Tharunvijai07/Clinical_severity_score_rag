CRITICAL CARE PROTOCOLS AND ICU MANAGEMENT GUIDELINES
Society of Critical Care Medicine (SCCM) — Integrated Reference

SECTION 1: ICU ADMISSION CRITERIA
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

LEVEL 1 — DIRECT ICU ADMISSION (immediate life-threatening):
  • Cardiopulmonary arrest requiring CPR
  • Respiratory failure requiring mechanical ventilation
  • Systolic BP < 90 mmHg despite fluid resuscitation (shock)
  • Altered mental status (GCS < 8) with aspiration risk
  • Acute MI with cardiogenic shock
  • Status epilepticus
  • Severe sepsis/septic shock (lactate > 4 mmol/L)
  • Multi-organ failure (≥ 2 organ systems failing)

LEVEL 2 — STEP-DOWN / HIGH-DEPENDENCY UNIT:
  • Hemodynamically unstable, monitoring required, not on vasopressors
  • Oxygen requirement > 40% FiO2 but no mechanical ventilation
  • Severe metabolic derangement with treatment initiated
  • Post-operative high-risk surgical patients

MULTI-ORGAN DYSFUNCTION SYNDROME (MODS) CRITERIA:
  Defined as failure of ≥ 2 of the following organ systems:
  • Respiratory: PaO2/FiO2 < 200 (on mechanical ventilation)
  • Cardiovascular: MAP < 70 mmHg or vasopressor requirement
  • Renal: Creatinine > 3.5 mg/dL or urine output < 0.5 mL/kg/h for 6 hours
  • Hepatic: Bilirubin > 6 mg/dL
  • Hematologic: Platelet count < 80,000/µL or INR > 2.0
  • Neurological: GCS < 10

  MODS mortality estimates:
    • 2 organs failing: ~30–40% mortality
    • 3 organs failing: ~60–70% mortality
    • 4 or more organs failing: > 80% mortality

SECTION 2: HEMODYNAMIC MONITORING AND TARGETS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BLOOD PRESSURE:
  • Mean Arterial Pressure (MAP) target in ICU: ≥ 65 mmHg
  • MAP < 65 mmHg despite vasopressors: Refractory shock — extremely high mortality
  • Systolic BP < 90 mmHg: Hypotension — evaluate for shock
  • Systolic BP < 70 mmHg: Severe hypotension — immediate resuscitation
  • Hypertensive emergency: SBP > 180 mmHg or MAP > 130 with end-organ damage

HEART RATE:
  • Normal: 60–100 beats/min
  • Tachycardia (> 100): Investigate fever, pain, hypovolemia, sepsis, arrhythmia
  • Severe tachycardia (> 150): Hemodynamic instability likely; cardioversion consideration
  • Bradycardia (< 50 with symptoms): Cardiac pacing may be required

RESPIRATORY RATE AND OXYGENATION:
  • Normal SpO2 target in ICU: 92–96% (avoid hyperoxia)
  • SpO2 < 90%: Hypoxemia — supplemental oxygen; consider mechanical ventilation
  • SpO2 < 85%: Critical hypoxemia — immediate intubation in most cases
  • Respiratory rate > 30 breaths/min: Impending respiratory failure
  • PaO2/FiO2 < 300: Mild ARDS
  • PaO2/FiO2 < 200: Moderate ARDS — high-flow oxygen or non-invasive ventilation
  • PaO2/FiO2 < 100: Severe ARDS — mechanical ventilation mandatory

TEMPERATURE:
  • Normothermia target: 36–37.5°C
  • Fever (> 38.3°C): Evaluate for infection; blood cultures; antibiotics if sepsis suspected
  • Hyperthermia (> 41°C): Malignant hyperthermia or heat stroke — medical emergency
  • Hypothermia (< 36°C): Assess for sepsis (particularly in elderly/immunocompromised), exposure

SECTION 3: GLYCEMIC CONTROL IN CRITICAL ILLNESS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BLOOD GLUCOSE TARGETS (ICU):
  • Target range: 140–180 mg/dL (NICE-SUGAR trial evidence)
  • Hypoglycemia (< 70 mg/dL): Dangerous; immediate dextrose administration
  • Severe hypoglycemia (< 40 mg/dL): Critical — may cause irreversible neuronal damage
  • Hyperglycemia (> 180 mg/dL): Insulin infusion protocol activation
  • Severe hyperglycemia (> 300 mg/dL): Assess for DKA or HHS
  • Extreme hyperglycemia (> 600 mg/dL): Hyperosmolar crisis — aggressive management

DIABETIC KETOACIDOSIS (DKA) CRITERIA:
  • Blood glucose > 250 mg/dL (sometimes lower in euglycemic DKA)
  • pH < 7.3
  • Serum bicarbonate < 18 mEq/L
  • Elevated anion gap (> 12)
  • Serum/urine ketones positive
  DKA Severity:
    Mild: pH 7.25–7.30; HCO3 15–18
    Moderate: pH 7.00–7.24; HCO3 10–15 (hospital admission)
    Severe: pH < 7.00; HCO3 < 10 (ICU required)

HYPEROSMOLAR HYPERGLYCEMIC STATE (HHS):
  • Glucose > 600 mg/dL
  • Serum osmolality > 320 mOsm/kg
  • Minimal ketoacidosis (pH > 7.3, HCO3 > 15)
  • Mortality 10–20%; requires ICU admission

SECTION 4: COAGULATION AND BLEEDING IN CRITICAL CARE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

COAGULATION REFERENCE RANGES:
  • PT: 11–13.5 seconds (INR 0.9–1.1 normal)
  • aPTT: 25–35 seconds
  • Fibrinogen: 200–400 mg/dL
  • D-dimer: < 500 ng/mL FEU

ANTICOAGULATION AND BLEEDING THRESHOLDS:
  • INR > 1.5: Elevated bleeding risk; FFP consideration before invasive procedures
  • INR > 2.0: Significant coagulopathy; restrict invasive procedures without correction
  • INR > 3.5: High bleeding risk; requires correction before surgery
  • Platelet < 50,000/µL: Platelet transfusion before procedures
  • Platelet < 10,000/µL: Spontaneous bleeding risk; prophylactic platelet transfusion

ANEMIA IN CRITICAL ILLNESS:
  • Hemoglobin (Hgb) target in ICU: 7–9 g/dL (restrictive transfusion strategy)
  • Hgb < 7 g/dL: RBC transfusion indicated (liberal strategy for ACS: < 8 g/dL)
  • Hgb < 5 g/dL: Critical anemia — immediate transfusion required
  • Hematocrit < 21%: Severe anemia — hemodynamic instability expected

SECTION 5: ACID-BASE DISORDERS IN CRITICAL CARE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ARTERIAL BLOOD GAS (ABG) REFERENCE RANGES:
  • pH: 7.35–7.45
  • PaO2: 80–100 mmHg
  • PaCO2: 35–45 mmHg
  • HCO3: 22–26 mEq/L
  • Base excess: -2 to +2 mEq/L

METABOLIC ACIDOSIS:
  • pH < 7.35 + low HCO3: Metabolic acidosis
  • Anion gap (AG) = Na – (Cl + HCO3); Normal: 8–12 mEq/L
  • Elevated AG (> 12): MUDPILES causes (Methanol, Uremia, DKA, Propylene glycol, Isoniazid, Lactic acidosis, Ethylene glycol, Salicylates)
  • pH < 7.2: Severe acidemia — consider bicarbonate therapy
  • pH < 7.1: Critical acidemia — mechanical ventilation often required

METABOLIC ALKALOSIS:
  • pH > 7.45 + elevated HCO3: Metabolic alkalosis
  • Common causes: Vomiting, diuretic use, nasogastric drainage

RESPIRATORY ACIDOSIS:
  • pH < 7.35 + elevated PaCO2: Respiratory acidosis — hypoventilation
  • PaCO2 > 55 mmHg with altered mental status: Ventilatory failure

RESPIRATORY ALKALOSIS:
  • pH > 7.45 + low PaCO2: Hyperventilation (pain, anxiety, PE, early sepsis)
