SEPSIS AND SEPTIC SHOCK CLINICAL GUIDELINES
Sepsis-3 Definitions, SOFA Scoring, and Management Protocols

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 1: SEPSIS DEFINITIONS (SEPSIS-3, 2016)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SEPSIS:
  Definition: Life-threatening organ dysfunction caused by a dysregulated host
  response to infection.
  Diagnostic Criterion: Suspected infection + acute change in total SOFA score ≥ 2 points
  (assumed baseline SOFA score of 0 in patients without pre-existing organ dysfunction)

SEPTIC SHOCK:
  Definition: Subset of sepsis with circulatory and cellular/metabolic dysfunction
  associated with greater risk of mortality.
  Diagnostic Criteria (both required):
    • Vasopressor requirement to maintain MAP ≥ 65 mmHg
    • Serum lactate > 2 mmol/L despite adequate fluid resuscitation
  Mortality: > 40% in-hospital

SYSTEMIC INFLAMMATORY RESPONSE SYNDROME (SIRS) — historical, no longer definitional:
  Two or more of:
    • Temperature > 38°C or < 36°C
    • Heart rate > 90 beats/min
    • Respiratory rate > 20 breaths/min or PaCO2 < 32 mmHg
    • WBC > 12,000/µL or < 4,000/µL or > 10% bands

SECTION 2: SOFA SCORE (SEQUENTIAL ORGAN FAILURE ASSESSMENT)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The SOFA score grades dysfunction in 6 organ systems (0–4 per system):

1. RESPIRATION (PaO2/FiO2 ratio):
   • ≥ 400: Score 0 (normal)
   • 300–399: Score 1 (mild impairment)
   • 200–299: Score 2 (moderate, with respiratory support)
   • 100–199: Score 3 (severe, with respiratory support)
   • < 100: Score 4 (critical)

2. COAGULATION (Platelets ×10³/µL):
   • ≥ 150: Score 0
   • 100–149: Score 1
   • 50–99: Score 2
   • 20–49: Score 3
   • < 20: Score 4 (critical thrombocytopenia — major bleeding risk)

3. LIVER (Bilirubin mg/dL):
   • < 1.2: Score 0 (normal)
   • 1.2–1.9: Score 1
   • 2.0–5.9: Score 2 (moderate hepatic dysfunction)
   • 6.0–11.9: Score 3
   • > 12.0: Score 4 (critical hepatic failure)

4. CARDIOVASCULAR (MAP and vasopressors):
   • MAP ≥ 70 mmHg: Score 0
   • MAP < 70 mmHg: Score 1
   • Dopamine ≤ 5 or dobutamine: Score 2
   • Dopamine 5.1–15 or norepinephrine ≤ 0.1: Score 3
   • Dopamine > 15 or norepinephrine > 0.1: Score 4

5. CENTRAL NERVOUS SYSTEM (Glasgow Coma Scale):
   • GCS 15: Score 0
   • GCS 13–14: Score 1
   • GCS 10–12: Score 2
   • GCS 6–9: Score 3
   • GCS < 6: Score 4

6. RENAL (Creatinine mg/dL / Urine output):
   • Creatinine < 1.2: Score 0
   • Creatinine 1.2–1.9: Score 1
   • Creatinine 2.0–3.4: Score 2
   • Creatinine 3.5–4.9 OR UO < 500 mL/day: Score 3
   • Creatinine > 5.0 OR UO < 200 mL/day: Score 4

SOFA TOTAL SCORE INTERPRETATION:
  • SOFA 0–6: Low mortality (< 10%)
  • SOFA 7–9: Moderate mortality (~15–20%)
  • SOFA 10–12: High mortality (~40–50%)
  • SOFA ≥ 13: Very high mortality (> 80%)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 3: qSOFA (QUICK SOFA — BEDSIDE SCREENING TOOL)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

qSOFA Score (1 point each):
  • Altered mentation (GCS < 15)
  • Respiratory rate ≥ 22 breaths/min
  • Systolic blood pressure ≤ 100 mmHg

qSOFA ≥ 2: Warrants further organ dysfunction assessment; associated with higher
risk of prolonged ICU stay or in-hospital death.

SECTION 4: LABORATORY BIOMARKERS IN SEPSIS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

LACTATE (serum):
  • Normal: < 2.0 mmol/L
  • Mildly elevated: 2.0–3.9 mmol/L — indicates tissue hypoperfusion; monitor
  • Septic shock criterion: Lactate > 2.0 mmol/L after adequate resuscitation
  • Severe hyperlactatemia: Lactate > 4.0 mmol/L — severe tissue ischemia; ICU mandatory
  • Lactate > 8.0 mmol/L: Critical; associated with high mortality
  Serial lactate monitoring: Lactate clearance goal > 10% per 2 hours during resuscitation

PROCALCITONIN (PCT):
  • Normal: < 0.1 ng/mL
  • PCT 0.1–0.5: Low-grade infection or inflammation
  • PCT 0.5–2.0: Moderate systemic inflammation; possible sepsis
  • PCT > 2.0: High probability of sepsis
  • PCT > 10.0: Severe sepsis/septic shock; bacteremia likely

C-REACTIVE PROTEIN (CRP):
  • Normal: < 1.0 mg/dL (< 10 mg/L)
  • Mildly elevated: 1–5 mg/dL — non-specific inflammation
  • Moderately elevated: 5–20 mg/dL — significant inflammation; infection probable
  • Severely elevated: > 20 mg/dL — severe systemic infection; sepsis likely

WHITE BLOOD CELL COUNT (WBC):
  • Normal: 4,500–11,000/µL
  • Leukocytosis: WBC > 12,000/µL — infection response
  • Severe leukocytosis: WBC > 30,000/µL — sepsis; leukemoid reaction
  • Leukopenia: WBC < 4,000/µL — immunosuppression; severe sepsis (poor prognosis)
  Bands (immature neutrophils) > 10%: Left shift — active bacterial infection

PLATELETS:
  • Normal: 150,000–400,000/µL
  • Thrombocytopenia: < 150,000/µL — monitor
  • Significant: < 100,000/µL — bleeding risk; evaluate for DIC
  • Critical: < 50,000/µL — spontaneous bleeding risk; platelet transfusion consideration
  • < 20,000/µL: Severe; immediate intervention

DISSEMINATED INTRAVASCULAR COAGULATION (DIC) IN SEPSIS:
  • Elevated D-dimer (> 500 ng/mL FEU)
  • Prolonged PT/INR (INR > 1.5)
  • Prolonged aPTT
  • Thrombocytopenia (< 100,000/µL)
  • Low fibrinogen (< 200 mg/dL)
  DIC Score (ISTH) ≥ 5: Overt DIC — life-threatening coagulopathy

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 5: SURVIVING SEPSIS CAMPAIGN — HOUR-1 BUNDLE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Within 1 hour of sepsis recognition:
  1. Measure lactate; re-measure if initial lactate > 2 mmol/L
  2. Obtain blood cultures before administering antibiotics
  3. Administer broad-spectrum antibiotics
  4. Begin rapid fluid resuscitation: 30 mL/kg IV crystalloid for hypotension or lactate ≥ 4 mmol/L
  5. Apply vasopressors if MAP < 65 mmHg during/after fluid resuscitation (norepinephrine first-line)

SEPTIC SHOCK RESUSCITATION TARGETS:
  • MAP ≥ 65 mmHg
  • CVP 8–12 mmHg (12–15 if mechanically ventilated)
  • ScvO2 ≥ 70%
  • Urine output ≥ 0.5 mL/kg/h
  • Lactate normalisation (< 2 mmol/L)
